MINE SPRITE SHEETS

JOSEPH No. 1 · grid templates · art check Control panel Play Day image outlines
Paint straight onto a template. Each cell has a 12px label strip and a 1px frame, and the game never reads either. It reads only the inside of each frame.
Magenta (#FF00FF) is guide colour and gets ignored, so leave it or paint over it. A cell you leave empty keeps the built-in art. Fill cells one at a time.
Save finished sheets as img/mine/walls.png, flats.png, sprites.png, tools.png and face.png. Optional glow sheets use the same grid: walls_glow.png etc. (white = lights itself).
The reference sheets are the built-in art on the same grid, if you'd rather paint over them than start blank. Ready-made copies of both are in art/mine/templates/ and art/mine/reference/.
